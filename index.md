# TnCPicks Privacy Policy

**Need to delete your TnCPicks account?**
[Request account deletion](#account-and-data-deletion)

You can request deletion even if you've uninstalled the app.

**Last updated: November 2, 2026**

TnCPicks is an NFL prediction application that allows users to create leagues, invite other players, submit game picks, and compare results.

This policy explains how information is handled when you use TnCPicks.

## Information We Collect

TnCPicks processes the following information to provide its services:

- **Account information:** Your email address, account identifier, and authentication information.
- **Profile information:** Your chosen display name.
- **League information:** Leagues you create or join, membership information, invitations, and commissioner roles.
- **Game predictions:** Your submitted picks, league standings, and related scoring information.
- **Technical information:** Information needed for authentication, security, troubleshooting, and operation of the service. Hosting providers may process network information such as IP addresses.

## How We Use Information

We use this information to authenticate users, maintain accounts, operate leagues, record predictions, calculate results, support league participation, and maintain service security.

## Information Shared With Other Users

Your display name, league participation, submitted picks, and standings may be visible to other members of your leagues.

Your account email address is used for account administration and authentication and is not intended to be displayed in league standings.

## Service Providers

TnCPicks uses **Supabase** for authentication, database storage, and backend services.

Supabase processes information as necessary to operate these services.

We may also use infrastructure providers to deliver and maintain the application.

## Data Security

TnCPicks uses authenticated access, encrypted network connections, and database access controls to help protect user information.

No online service can guarantee absolute security.

## Data Retention

Account information, profiles, league participation, and predictions are maintained as needed to provide TnCPicks.

When account deletion is completed, the associated authentication account and application data are deleted.

Limited deletion-request records, such as an account identifier, request status, and timestamps, may be retained for operational integrity, security, and confirmation of deletion.

Other information may be retained when required by applicable law.

## Account and Data Deletion

You may request permanent account deletion using either method below.

### Delete your account within TnCPicks

Sign in, open **My Profile**, select **Delete Account**, and confirm the deletion.

This removes your account and associated application data. League ownership may be transferred or a league may be removed when appropriate.

### Request deletion without the app

If you cannot access TnCPicks or have already uninstalled it, email:

**[frognarrok@gmail.com](mailto:frognarrok@gmail.com?subject=TnCPicks%20Account%20Deletion%20Request)**

Use the subject **TnCPicks Account Deletion Request** and provide the email address associated with your TnCPicks account.

We may request reasonable verification of account ownership before processing your request. Never send your password.

You can use this email-based process without reinstalling the app.

## Your Privacy Questions

For questions about your information, requests for assistance, or account deletion, contact:

**[frognarrok@gmail.com](mailto:frognarrok@gmail.com)**

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy as TnCPicks evolves. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
